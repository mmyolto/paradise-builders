About PBS

A Papua New Guinean builder, owned and run in PNG

Professional and semi-professional staff, our own plant and equipment, and decades of building experience across the country.

Our story

From a highlands hardware supplier to a national contractor

Paradise Building Systems is nationally owned and operated, registered under Papua New Guinea law through the Investment Promotion Authority.

1995Registered as Southern Highlands Hardware Supplies Limited

The company begins as a building supplies business in the Southern Highlands.

28 April 2000Becomes Paradise Building Systems

The operating name changes to Paradise Building Systems as the business moves into construction.

GrowthOil & gas project sites

Under the Managing Director’s leadership, PBS delivers building projects on major oil and gas sites to client specification and on schedule.

TodayPort Moresby & Lae

Project management, engineering and construction from two bases, with our own plant and specialist labour.

What drives us

Vision, mission, values and objectives

Vision

To build long-term relationships based on integrity, performance, value and client satisfaction, and to earn the privilege of being the contractor of choice for steel-frame, timber and civil earthworks construction.

Mission

To provide the highest quality steel-frame and timber construction services in a safe, professionally managed project environment, deliver quality craftsmanship with courtesy and respect, and keep a workplace that grows careers and teamwork.

Values

Compliance with all legislation, codes of practice and contract specifications. Workplace health and safety. Quality work that stays cost-effective. These are upheld at every level of the company and in every business relationship.

Objectives

  1. Follow best practice in all construction and maintenance work.
  2. Lead the industry through employee performance and continuous site training.
  3. Meet client goals through sound methodology, procurement and delivery.
  4. Keep every site incident-free while delivering quality-assured projects.
